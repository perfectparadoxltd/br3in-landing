How it works

01

Verify You're a Dentist

Sign up with your GDC, NPI, AHPRA, or equivalent professional registration. We verify against official registries before activating your account. No imposters.

02

Install the Mac App

Signed, notarized macOS app (under 50MB). Drop in your clinical notes, case studies, protocols, and literature references. BR3IN.Dental indexes everything semantically on your machine.

03

Pick Your AI

Connect to ChatGPT, Claude, or any cloud LLM you already use. Or install Ollama for fully offline AI on your Mac — patient data never leaves the building.

04

Optionally, Share With Peers

Share short citations, summaries, and your own anonymized observations with other verified dentists. Patient identifiers are blocked. Pirated content is blocked. Only your knowledge, properly credited.

Frequently Asked

Is patient data really safe? +
Yes. BR3IN.Dental is local-first: your indexed data sits on YOUR Mac, encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM). The upload pipeline blocks patient identifiers (names, DOB, addresses, NHS numbers, IDs) before they ever touch the vector database. For maximum privacy when querying, use Mode C (local Llama via Ollama) — patient data never leaves your machine, period.
Do I need to install an AI to use this? +
No. BR3IN.Dental works in 3 modes: (A) Pure semantic search of your library — no AI needed; (B) Connect to your existing ChatGPT / Claude / Gemini account; (C) Install Ollama (free, 1-click) for fully local AI. You pick. Most dentists use Mode B for non-clinical work and Mode C for anything touching patient data.
How is this different from ChatGPT Pro? +
ChatGPT was trained on the open web up to a cutoff date. BR3IN.Dental gives ANY LLM (including ChatGPT itself, if you use Mode B) access to your clinical knowledge plus verified peer contributions in dentistry specifically. Your case notes, your protocols, recent literature your colleagues have curated. ChatGPT doesn't know any of that. You can use both together.
What can I legally share with peers? +
Your own clinical notes (anonymized — no patient identifiers, ever). Your own case studies, observations, and protocols. Short quotes from copyrighted literature WITH proper citation (fair use, max ~500 words per source). Summaries and translations of public material. References, DOIs, and links. You may NOT upload full PDFs, textbooks, pirated content, or any patient identifiable data. Our upload pipeline enforces this in code.
How do you verify dentists? +
For UK dentists, we cross-check your registration number against the GDC (General Dental Council) public register in under a minute. For US, we use the NPI Registry. For Australia, AHPRA. For other countries, you upload a scan of your professional license and we review manually within 48 hours. Only verified dentists can join, query, or share — no anonymous accounts.
How is this different from PubMed or Sci-Hub? +
PubMed gives you abstracts (free) and full papers (often paywalled). Sci-Hub gives you pirated papers (illegal in many jurisdictions). BR3IN.Dental is neither — it's a place where verified dentists share short citations, summaries, case studies, and their own anonymized observations, all properly attributed and legally compliant. Think Reddit + Wikipedia for the dental profession, with AI search built in.
